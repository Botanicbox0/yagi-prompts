# Photo-craft for Seedream 5.0 Pro and Nano Banana Pro

Write prose, not tags. The models learned from photos, so describe what you would see, not the physics.
**Light formula**: [modifier + size] + [side and height] + [distance] + [what it does to the shadow and the highlight]. For example: "a large strip softbox camera-left, close, slightly behind, drawing one long highlight down the bottle edge".

## 1. Light

**Hard vs soft depends on how large the source looks from the subject.** A closer light is softer and falls off faster. Inverse square law: twice the distance gives a quarter of the light.

| | Hard | Soft |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Bare flash, sun, fresnel, small and far | Big softbox close, overcast sky, diffused window |
| Shadow / specular | Crisp edge, small bright highlight | Gradual gradient, broad wrapping highlight |
| Texture | Exaggerated | Smoothed |

| Setup | Placement → look |
|---|---|
| Key / fill | The key sets direction; fill on the opposite side sets shadow depth |
| Rim / kicker | Behind and to the side → edge line on the jaw or bottle |
| Back | Directly behind → glass and liquid glow |
| Top | Overhead → gradient down the bottle shoulder, eye sockets go dark |
| Butterfly | High, in front → shadow under the nose; add a bounce below for **clamshell** |
| Rembrandt | 45° to the side, 45° up → triangle of light on the shadow cheek |
| Side (split) | 90° → half the face lit, strong texture |
| Under | Weak bounce opens the eyes; at full power it's horror |

| Modifier | Signature |
|---|---|
| Softbox / octa | Even light; rectangular or round catchlight; octa wraps more |
| Strip | Long specular line, **the standard for bottle edges** |
| Beauty dish | Crisp centre, fast falloff; cheekbones, glossy lips |
| Umbrella | Broad, spills everywhere |
| Bounce (V-flat, card) | Soft fill: "white card camera-right" |
| Scrim / silk | Softens sun or strobe: "sun through a 1/4 grid-cloth" |
| Flag / negative fill | Black card that removes light; **dark edges on glass** |
| Gobo / cookie | Patterned shadow: window frame, blinds, leaves |

| Natural / flash | Character |
|---|---|
| Morning window | Low, clean, slightly cool |
| Midday sun | High, hard, short black shadows |
| Overcast / north window | Giant softbox, low contrast, about 6,500 K |
| Golden hour | Low, about 3,500 K, long shadows, warm rim |
| Blue hour | Cool ambient; pair with warm lamps |
| On-camera flash | Flat, hard drop shadow, hotspots, dark falloff |
| Ring flash | Shadowless, halo on the wall, donut catchlights |

- **Colour**: tungsten 3,200 K, daylight 5,600 K, shade 7,000 K+. CTO warms, CTB cools. Gel the background or rim, not the key. Motivate mixed light ("warm lamp against cool window").
- **Specular vs diffuse**: a specular highlight mirrors the source's shape (strip → line, octa → circle) and appears only from the *family of angles* (*Light: Science & Magic*). Diffuse reflection carries colour and texture. To get gloss, shape the source instead of writing "glossy".
- **Glass**: *bright-field* (white background, black flags) gives dark edges; *dark-field* (black background, side-back lights) gives white edges.
- **Caustics** need **hard** light through glass or liquid. Soft light kills them.
- **Contrast**: 2:1 beauty, 3–4:1 editorial, 8:1 chiaroscuro. Phrase it as "shadow side one stop darker, holding detail".

## 2. Texture and rendering

| Material | Phrase that reads as real |
|---|---|
| Clear glass | "background bends through the walls; faint green in the thick base" |
| Tinted or smoked glass | "darker at base and edges, lighter in thin walls, visible liquid line" |
| Frosted glass | "acid-etched, glows where light passes" |
| Thick base | "heavy base magnifies and flips the plinth edge" |
| Brushed metal | "linear brushing, highlight streak across the grain" |
| Polished metal | "chrome reflecting white cards and one black flag" (design what it reflects) |
| Anodised metal | "bead-blasted anodised aluminium, soft sheen" |
| Satin and silk | "highlight rolling along the folds, soft creases" |
| Matte plastic | "soft-touch matte, faint parting line" |
| Ceramic | "glaze pooling at the foot, orange-peel waviness"; bisque reads "chalky" |
| Wood | "oiled walnut, open pores" |
| Paper | "uncoated stock, letterpress bite, corner curl" |
| Liquid and gel | "meniscus, trapped micro-bubbles, internal caustics" |
| Cream and balm | "spatula swipe with soft peaks, satin sheen" |
| Powder | "pressed pan, one crumbled edge, fallout specks" |
| Skin | "pores, peach fuzz catching the rim, uneven tone, translucent backlit fingertips" |

**Imperfections**: use one to three, marked "subtle". Dust on dark acrylic, a fingerprint seen only in the highlight, hairline scratches on the cap, uneven condensation with one trail, the cap slightly off axis, a mould seam, a lifting label edge, flyaway hairs.

| Avoid | Result | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| hyperrealistic, ultra-detailed, 8K, masterpiece | Generic AI look, over-sharpened | A camera, a lens and a stock |
| octane, Unreal, 3D render, CGI | Renders it literally | "photograph" plus the light setup |
| flawless, porcelain, airbrushed or glowing skin | Wax | "natural skin texture, light editorial retouch" |
| glossy or shiny with no source named | Gloss everywhere | The source shape and where its highlight lands |
| dramatic, cinematic or studio lighting | Generic | A named setup and modifier |
| pristine, perfectly symmetrical, vibrant, HDR | Sterile, oversaturated | One or two imperfections; "true-to-life saturation" |

Both vendors also advise positive phrasing: "empty street", not "no cars".

## 3. Optics

| Focal length (35 mm equiv.) | Effect |
|---|---|
| 24 mm | Exaggerates near objects, stretches the edges |
| 35 mm | Honest environmental view, lifestyle |
| 50 mm | Close to eye view; hands, tabletop |
| 85 mm | Flattering compression; portrait, beauty |
| 90–120 mm macro | No distortion, parallel lines; **product hero** |
| 135–200 mm | Flattened, background shrinks; fashion |

- **Aperture**: f/1.2–2 razor-thin focus, f/4 gentle falloff, f/8–16 catalogue-sharp. At 1:1 macro the depth of field is about 1 mm even at f/8, so write "focus-stacked" or name the focus plane.
- **Tilt-shift**: shift keeps tall bottles straight; tilt lays the focus plane along a tabletop.

| Lens | Signature |
|---|---|
| Helios 44-2 58/2 | Swirly, cat's-eye bokeh, low contrast, streaky flare |
| Summilux 50/1.4 pre-ASPH, wide open | Highlight glow (spherical aberration) |
| Zeiss Planar/Otus | High micro-contrast, "3D pop" |
| Canon 85 f/1.2L | Wafer-thin focus, creamy bokeh, faint purple fringing |
| Vintage uncoated | Veiling flare, lifted blacks, ghosts |

**Filters**: Black Pro-Mist 1/8–1/4 blooms highlights and slightly lifts shadows; 1/2 looks hazy. Glimmerglass adds glints and softens gently (Tiffen). Halation is a red-orange fringe on bright edges. Keep vignetting to about one stop and chromatic aberration *faint*.

## 4. Analogue and digital hardware

| Stock | Signature → use |
|---|---|
| Portra 160 | Fine grain, low contrast, pastel skin → beauty, white sets |
| Portra 400 | Warm, accurate skin, wide latitude → default for people |
| Portra 800 | Warmer, more grain → interiors, dusk |
| Ektar 100 | Finest grain, punchy colour → product; turns skin red |
| Fuji Pro 400H (discontinued) | Cool mint shadows, airy → bridal |
| Velvia 50 | Vivid slide film, deep blacks → colour-block still life; harsh on skin |
| CineStill 800T | Tungsten-balanced; red halation (remjet removed); cyan in daylight → night, neon |
| CineStill 50D | Daylight, fine grain, clean, slightly cool |
| HP5 / Tri-X / Delta 3200 | Classic / gritty and punchy / heavy B&W grain |
| Polaroid 600 / SX-70 | Soft, shifted colour, lifted blacks; **draws the white frame** |

**Cameras**: Hasselblad 500C/M means 6×6 with smooth tones (it may force a square crop). Mamiya RZ67 means 6×7 studio fashion. Pentax 67 (105/2.4) separates the subject. Contax T2 means a flash snapshot. Leica M6 means reportage. Fuji GFX and Phase One IQ4 give clean digital medium format, the IQ4 for commercial still life.

**Scanners** (Carmencita Film Lab): Frontier is warmer and punchier with denser blacks. Noritsu is more neutral and flatter, keeps more shadow detail, and skin leans magenta.

- **Hardware names help** when one stock plus one camera or lens defines the look, with real focal lengths and apertures. Replicate shows Seedream following "Leica M, 50mm Summilux wide open".
- **They hurt** when stacked, when they clash with the light (Velvia with pastels), when they draw props (Polaroid frames, Leica red dots), or when they put grain on a white sweep. A stock line inside an edit regrades the whole frame. For clean work, write "clean digital medium-format capture, no grain".

## 5. Still-life devices

| Device | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Plinth | "low honed travertine cylinder, 1.5× bottle width" |
| Acrylic block | "clear riser, edges catching a line of light" |
| Reflection | "glossy black acrylic, reflection fading within 3 cm" |
| Shadow | "hard sun, long diagonal shadow"; "gobo of leaves" |
| Props | One material echo (raw silk, citrus, stone), true to scale |
| Negative space | "product about 30% of frame, empty top-left for a headline" |
| Colour blocking | "sand floor against sage wall" |
| Hands | Enter from the frame edge, real grip, knuckle creases |
| Splash | "frozen by high-speed flash, crown splash, specular point on each droplet" |

## 6. Engines

**Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3 Pro Image)**
- **Structure**: a narrative paragraph; Google says keyword lists "won't cut it". Order: subject, action, location, composition, style. Template: "A photorealistic [shot] of [subject] in [setting]. [Light]. Shot from [angle] with [lens]." Name materials ("navy tweed", not "jacket").
- **References**: up to 14, with at most 6 high-fidelity objects, 5 characters and 3 style references. Give each a role ("Use Image A for the pose, Image B for the style").
- **Editing**: iterate over several turns: "change only [X]… keep everything else exactly the same, preserving the original style, lighting and composition." Google warns that lighting changes and complex edits can produce artefacts.
- **Strong at**: quoted text with a named font, Korean, logos on packaging "while preserving natural lighting and texture", 4K, 10 aspect ratios.
- **Weak at**: small faces, spelling and fine detail (DeepMind). In our use skin comes out with a polished sheen; counter it with texture and film-stock cues.

**Seedream 5.0 Pro** (Volcengine guide and API)
- **Structure**: concise natural language. **Subject + action + environment** first, then style, colour, light and composition, plus what the image is for. Concise and precise beats stacking ornate words. Stay **under 600 English words**, or elements get dropped. The start of the prompt weighs most (Runware).
- **References**: **up to 10**, named by number ("replace the subject of image 1 with the subject of image 2, in the style of image 3"). Say what to keep, then the new scene.
- **Editing**: add, delete, replace or modify one precisely named target, with no pronouns, and state what stays unchanged. Arrows, boxes or scribbles drawn on the input mark regions. You can also edit by coordinates and split an image into up to 16 layers.
- **Output**: 1K/1.5K/2K, **no 4K**. Describe the aspect ratio in the prompt. Text goes in double quotes and should stay short.
- **Strong at**: editorial looks, film and lens references, spatial precision. **Weak at**: scenes built from scratch, which read as "typical AI". It works best from a real reference frame plus a product cutout.

| Job | Pick |
|---|---|
| Product-only hero; product swapped into our own frame | **Seedream** ("image 1 frame, image 2 cutout, replace only the bottle") |
| Labels, Korean copy, logos, 4K, more than 10 references | **NB Pro** |
| Try-on with identity | NB Pro first; check skin and hands |
| Video keyframe | NB Pro if the label must survive, otherwise Seedream |

Higgsfield may cap references and resolutions below the API limits.

## 7. Prompt skeletons

**A. Hero perfume on white (Seedream)**
> Photorealistic hero still life for a fragrance landing page, vertical 4:5. A [smoked-glass] bottle with a [walnut pebble cap] and an etched "Yagi" script stands just right of centre on a low [honed white travertine] plinth. It fills about 30% of the frame, with white space above for a headline, on a warm-white sweep that darkens softly behind. A large strip softbox camera-left, slightly behind, lays a long vertical highlight down the shoulder. A narrow strip camera-right draws a thin edge line. Black flags on both sides give the glass crisp dark outlines. A small hard spot behind throws an amber caustic through the thick base onto the plinth. Short, soft shadow to the right. Add a faint mould seam and two dust specks on the cap. Phase One IQ4, 120 mm macro at f/11, camera at shoulder height, verticals parallel, clean grain-free capture, neutral grade.

**B. Hand entering frame**
> Close editorial product photo, 4:5. A woman's hand enters from the lower-right edge, wrist out of frame, holding a [frosted serum bottle with a black dropper] between thumb and two fingers. The hand should look real: knuckle creases, fine skin texture, short almond nails in sheer [milky] polish, a dry cuticle. Background: [sage linen] against [sand plaster], soft focus. Late-afternoon window light from camera-left passes through a thin sheer at about 4,000 K. A white card on the right holds the shadow side at one stop darker. The frosted glass glows where light passes and the fingertips look translucent. 90 mm at f/4, focus on the bottle shoulder and nearest fingertip. Portra 160 look.

**C. Beauty macro, lip oil**
> Extreme macro beauty shot, 1:1, near life-size. A [medium-olive] skinned model's lower lip fills the frame, mouth slightly parted. Fresh [rose-brown] lip oil sits thick at the centre and thins at the edges, pooling in the vertical lip lines, with one tiny trapped bubble. Fine creases, a dry fleck near the corner, pores, and peach fuzz above the lip that catches the light. A socked beauty dish high and straight ahead plus a white bounce below (clamshell) lays one broad, crisp specular band across the oil and small glints along the ridges. 100 mm macro at f/8, focus-stacked across the lip, surrounding skin in soft blur. Unsmoothed skin, true-to-life saturation.

**D. Fashion try-on (NB Pro)**
> Use Image 1 as the person: keep her face, hair, skin tone and proportions exactly. Use Image 2 as the garment: reproduce the [tan suede overshirt] with its exact colour, seams, buttons and nap. Full-length fashion editorial, 4:5. She stands in a [bare concrete stairwell], hand in pocket, weight on her back leg, looking past camera. The key is overcast daylight from a tall doorway camera-right. A black flag on the left keeps the shadow side two stops down. The suede creases at the elbow, the tee shows its weave, and her skin has pores and a few flyaway hairs. Mamiya RZ67, 110 mm at f/4, camera at waist height. The look of Portra 400 scanned on a Frontier.

**E. Product to video keyframe**
> Start frame for image-to-video, 9:16. The [Yagi bottle] stands on [smoked acrylic] in the lower-centre third, about 25% of frame height. Above it, an empty dark-graphite gradient leaves room for a push-in and rising mist. A thin hard backlight from above and behind rims the shoulders and glows through the amber liquid. A narrow strip camera-left lays one vertical highlight on the left shoulder. The acrylic reflection fades within a few centimetres, and a few condensation beads sit on the lower glass. Everything is crisp and frozen, the air clear, the frame free of type. 85 mm at f/5.6, camera level at shoulder height. CineStill 50D look.
> *(Video prompt only: slow 10% dolly-in, the highlight sweeps across the glass, mist drifts in from the left. Keep motion out of the image prompt.)*
