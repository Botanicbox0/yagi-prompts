## Hosted ComfyUI (Comfy Cloud)
- ToS (effective 2026-05-13, comfy.org/terms-of-service): customer keeps Input and Output (defined as visual content; audio not named); "Comfy will not use Input or Output to train generative AI or diffusion models." No clause about open-model licences, so a model's own licence still applies wherever it runs (inference).
- Pricing (comfy.org/cloud/pricing): plans $20 / $35 / $100 a month; GPU billed by the second; RTX PRO 6000 96GB $3.49/hr; run limit 30 min (Pro 1 h). API needs a paid subscription (`https://cloud.comfy.org`, `X-API-Key`).
- RunPod list (runpod.io/pricing): A6000 48GB $0.69/hr, L40S $1.09, A100 80GB $2.09, H100 $2.89, RTX PRO 6000 96GB $2.69.

## Legal background
- YouTube Content ID (support.google.com/youtube/answer/2605065): reference files need exclusive rights; non-exclusively licensed content and production loops are ineligible. AI output is not mentioned. A claim on a generated track usually comes from someone else registering the same audio; defence = generation record + licence terms forbidding registration.
- US Copyright Office Part 2 (2025-01-29): "Copyright does not extend to purely AI-generated material".
- Korea Copyright Commission registration guide (2025-06-30), p.13: output with no human creative contribution is not a work and cannot be registered.
- Korea AI Basic Act art. 31(2): a provider of a generative-AI service must indicate that the output was generated by generative AI; 31(3) clear notice for realistic synthetic sound, image or video. Fine up to KRW 30M is listed for 31(1) only; grace period of a year or more reported 2026-01-22. Enforcement decree art. 23 text: 확인 못 함.
- Label lawsuits: UMG–Udio (2025-10), WMG–Udio and WMG–Suno (2025-11) settled into licensed platforms; UMG and Sony v. Suno reported stalled in 2026-04; nothing later found.
