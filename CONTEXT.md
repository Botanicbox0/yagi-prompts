# TWINGS campaign vocabulary

- **Brand workspace**: A private organization whose invited members can request campaigns, review creative work and follow results.
- **Campaign request**: A brand's proposed objective, audience, budget and schedule. Submitting one does not execute a contract or payment.
- **Studio**: The TWINGS team that manages production, published links, evidence and reporting.
- **Views**: The sum of each post's latest known view count. Repeat viewing may be included; this is not the number of unique people reached.
- **Engagement ranking**: Posts ranked by likes plus comments, limited to posts where both counts are known. Shares and saves remain separate metrics.
- **Engagement per view**: Likes plus comments divided by views, using only posts where all three figures are known and views are positive.
- **Published report**: A dated, immutable selection of campaign results. Corrections require a new version.
- **Unknown measurement**: A missing, unavailable or private figure. It is distinct from a measured zero.
